Connecting theoretical models to reality

I am a computational physicist and postdoc at the Facility for Rare Isotope Beams, in the Bayesian Analysis of Nuclear Dynamics (BAND) collaboration and the Few-Body Reaction Theory group. I am interested in connecting ideas across fields to answer big scientific questions: how can we describe nuclear reaction dynamics across resolution scales from first principles? How can we take all sources of uncertainty into account, including theoretical, when inferring natural properties from experiment?

Addressing these questions often involves reduced-order and surrogate models, Bayesian model calibration, machine learning and AI, high-performance computing, and ideas and tools from across theoretical and experimental physics.

I did my PhD (2024) in Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences at the University of Michigan, and have had the opportunity to work across fields and laboratories, including LANL, LLNL, PNNL, and CERN.

    Software

    jitr
    GPU-accelerated, just-in-time compiled (JAX) direct nuclear reaction solver toolkit built on the calculable R-matrix method.
    cosmo_gpu
    GPU (CUDA/CuPy) accelerated block-Lanczos backend for the CoSMo shell-model eigensolver.
    rxmc
    Orchestration layer for hierarchical Bayesian modeling of nuclear reactions.
    black-box-bayes
    Driver layer for open-source samplers used in Bayesian model calibration.
    exfor_tools
    Toolkit for parsing and cleaning experimental data from EXFOR for model training.
    nook
    Nuclear structure data from ENSDF and RIPL-3. Documentation
    ROSE
    Reduced-basis surrogate/emulator toolkit for elastic scattering. bandframework/rose
    Bfrescox
    Python wrapper for the Frescox coupled-reaction-channels code. bandframework/Bfrescox
    breakup-calibration-demo
    Emulates Frescox CDCC calculations with surmise and Bfrescox to infer the 8B asymptotic normalization coefficient from breakup cross sections.

    ROSE, Bfrescox and breakup-calibration-demo are part of the BAND framework.
